# ADR-012: Business onboarding

- **City is chosen at draft creation** (`city_id` in `POST /provider/businesses`) because `Business.city` and `Business.location` are required columns shared with the public side. The pin starts at the city centre; the `location` step must be completed explicitly (tracked in `onboarding_progress`) before submission.
- **Cities**: active and `visibility != hidden` (preview cities are allowed, so pilots can onboard before launch). Pins must lie within `CITY_MAX_RADIUS_KM` (default 60) of the city centre (haversine).
- **Step payloads** are validated per step with strict (no extra fields) schemas; a step is "done" from real data, not only from the progress flag, so the checklist stays truthful when data is later removed. Services, photos and documents are created through their own endpoints; the `services` step may also create services from starter-menu templates (duplicates by name skipped).
- **Required to submit**: basics (name, description), explicit location + address + phone, >= 1 active service, weekly business hours, logo + 1..20 photos, documents `cnic_front`, `cnic_back`, `selfie_with_cnic` (licence and storefront optional). A newly uploaded document replaces earlier pending/rejected ones of the same kind; accepted documents are kept and locked.
- **Editing is allowed in `draft`, `needs_info` and `rejected`**; `pending` is locked (409 `business_locked`).
- **Resubmission**: after `needs_info` unlimited; after `rejected` allowed **once** (a new `BusinessSubmission` row flagged `is_resubmission_after_rejection`); a second rejection is final (409 `resubmission_not_allowed`).
- **Terms**: provider agreement + commission terms must both be accepted and `terms_version` must equal `PROVIDER_TERMS_VERSION`; version, time and IP are stored per submission.
- **Private documents** live in the `private` storage alias (own folder/bucket, no public URL: `url()` raises). Access is by signed, short-lived (`PRIVATE_URL_TTL_SECONDS`, default 300 s) application links `/api/v1/files/documents/{token}`, issued only to the owner (admins in Plan 6.3 via the same `signed_url` helper). Content type comes from magic bytes plus decoding (images) or `%%EOF` (PDF), never from the filename.
- **Starter menu** templates are seeded with `manage.py seed_starter_menu` (idempotent; creates the standard top-level categories if missing) instead of a migration, because categories are admin-managed data.
