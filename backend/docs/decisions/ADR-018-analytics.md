# ADR-018: Basic analytics

- **Where**: models, jobs and queries live in the `bookings` app (they are derived from appointments and need their models); no new app, so no new import contract.
- **Bucketing**: by the business-local date of the appointment **start** (`TruncDate(..., tzinfo=business tz)`), so a 00:30 or 23:30 PKT booking lands on its Karachi day, never on the UTC day.
- **Definitions**: `bookings_total` counts every appointment that day (all statuses); `completed`, `cancelled_by_customer`, `cancelled_by_business`, `no_shows` count by status; `revenue_completed` is the sum of `price_at_booking` of the items of **completed** appointments only (the stored snapshot, so later price edits never change history; no discount concept exists yet, so gross == net). `ServiceDailyStats.bookings` counts items on non-cancelled appointments, `revenue` the completed items' `price_at_booking`; per day the service revenues add up to the business revenue (tested as an invariant).
- **One writer**: `analytics.recompute_range(business, start, end)` deletes and re-inserts the rows of the range from the appointments under a per-business advisory lock. The nightly task (`rebuild_daily_stats`, 02:10 Asia/Karachi, yesterday + today of every business) and its manual form (`from_date`, `to_date`, optional `business_id`) call it, so re-running never double counts and rows for days that no longer have data disappear.
- **Same-day freshness**: instead of fragile `+1` increments, every booking event (created, confirmed, completed, cancelled, no-show, rescheduled - including the old day of a reschedule) recomputes the touched day through the same function. It cannot drift from the appointments and is cheap (one day, two grouped queries). Failures are logged (raised when `ANALYTICS_RAISE_ON_ERROR`).
- **Ranges** (business-local): `today`; `week` = Monday to today; `month` = 1st to today; `30d` = last 30 days including today; `custom` = inclusive `from`/`to` (<= 366 days). Summary returns the totals, the immediately preceding period of the same length, cancellation and no-show rates (of all bookings) and the average completed booking value. Time series are zero-filled; weekly points start on Monday (the first one is clipped to the range). `top-services` ranks by count (ties by revenue) or revenue and lists only services with a non-zero value; limit 1-20.
- **CSV export** (<= 366 days, 10 per hour): a streamed generator over `queryset.iterator(chunk_size=500)` with one contact lookup per chunk. Cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or CR get a leading `'` (so phone numbers appear as `'+92...`). The customer phone column follows the dashboard rule: only for active, upcoming bookings.
