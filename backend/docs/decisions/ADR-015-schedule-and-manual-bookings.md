# ADR-015: Blocks, manual bookings and conflicts

- **Blocks reuse `Closure`** (kind `block` / `leave` / `holiday`, optional `staff`). `POST .../blocks` takes timezone-aware datetimes (part-day or multi-day, at most 366 days); `POST .../closures` (Step 2) remains the whole-day shortcut. Both go through the same cache invalidation, so public availability reflects a block or its removal immediately.
- **Edits never cancel bookings.** Adding a block or changing hours only makes `GET .../conflicts` list future pending/confirmed appointments that are now outside the resource's working hours (`outside_hours`, a booking must fit inside one shift, staff override hours apply in staff mode) or overlapping a block (`blocked`). Resolving a conflict is a reschedule or cancel (Step 5).
- **Manual bookings** use the same safety rules as online ones: business row lock, staff row lock (staff mode requires a capable `staff_id`), the shared `window_problems` check (hours, blocks, existing bookings and live holds, capacity or per-staff) and the Postgres exclusion constraint. Differences: the start may be any minute (not only the slot grid), lead time/advance rules do not apply, and a start up to 12 hours in the past (logging a walk-in just after it began) or `max_advance_days + 1` ahead is accepted. Errors: 409 `slot_unavailable` (busy), 409 `time_blocked`, 422 `outside_working_hours`.
- **Customer link**: `Appointment.customer` is now nullable. A manual booking links to an existing active account only when that account's phone equals the entered number **and is verified**; otherwise it stores `walk_in_name` / `walk_in_phone`. Walk-ins get no notifications and earn no points; linked customers get the normal `booking_confirmed` message. The owner is not alerted about their own manual booking (`booking_created` carries `source="manual"`).
- **Schedule** (`GET .../schedule?from=&to=&staff_id=`) uses inclusive local dates in the business timezone, at most 62 days (422 beyond), returns weekly hours, staff override hours, blocks, appointments (all statuses; an appointment crossing midnight appears on both days) and the number of live holds. Customer phones are hidden for non-active bookings.
- `Appointment.business_note` (owner-private text) is added now and used by the dashboard in Step 5.
